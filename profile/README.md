## Who we are

We are a community of builders who believe AI is not just a tool, but a **partner** in the engineering process. Our goal is to establish high standards for AI-assisted development, foster a culture of sharing, and build the future of collaborative AI engineering together.

We operate on the principles of **ODS (Open Data Science)**: transparency, horizontal connections, and peer verification. We value contribution over seniority and results over rhetoric.
